# RC23 Excise Tax Control Policy

1. Goods must be registered against an excise category before warehouse movements are recorded.
2. The taxable unit value is the greater of registered retail price and indicative price held in the master record.
3. Tax-suspension movements do not create a payable journal unless the movement is explicitly classified as payable or customs-paid.
4. Release for consumption, self-consumption and unexplained loss require PAYABLE settlement.
5. Movement approval requires a user independent from the creator.
6. Outbound and suspended-transfer quantities cannot exceed approved warehouse stock.
7. Returns cover complete two-month periods beginning in January, March, May, July, September or November.
8. A return cannot be submitted while its payable amount differs from the related GL excise liability.
9. The bank-guarantee calculation is an internal indicator and does not replace the licensing determination of the authority.
10. Official filing, customs reconciliation and tax-stamp integrations remain external controlled activities.
