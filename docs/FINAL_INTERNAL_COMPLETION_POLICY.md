# Final Internal Completion Control Policy

The final completion workbench is the control layer for advanced costing, planning, period close, financial drill-down and evidence-based readiness.

- Cost runs require independent review and approval before posting.
- Variance journals are balanced and linked to an immutable analysis hash.
- Planning scenarios are versioned; approved scenarios may be frozen and cannot be silently overwritten.
- Unified close cannot complete while mandatory checks fail or while the checklist hash changes after review.
- Readiness separates internal mandatory checks, production evidence, and external credentials.
- External credentials are never treated as internally completed merely because an interface exists.
- Production approval requires signed evidence references, not narrative assertions.
