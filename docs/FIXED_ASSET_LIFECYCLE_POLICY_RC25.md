# CORVAX RC25 — Fixed Asset Lifecycle Policy

RC25 completes the controlled lifecycle of fixed assets after acquisition and depreciation.

## Supported transactions
- Transfer between branches, cost centres and custodians.
- Impairment and impairment reversal with recoverable-amount evidence.
- Classification as held for sale and reversal of that classification.
- Full or partial sale with VAT treatment and gain/loss recognition.
- Disposal and write-off with derecognition of cost, accumulated depreciation and accumulated impairment.

## Core controls
- Draft, submit and independent approve/post workflow.
- The creator or submitter cannot approve the same transaction.
- A held-for-sale asset is excluded from depreciation runs.
- Partial disposals allocate cost, accumulated depreciation and impairment proportionately.
- Each posted transaction retains its journal, user, date, reason and reference.
- Assets with history are never deleted; final states are retained in the register.

## Production boundary
This internal engine does not replace independent valuation, approved disposal minutes, tax advice, or physical custody evidence.
