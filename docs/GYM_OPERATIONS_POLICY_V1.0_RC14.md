# CORVAX RC14 — Gym Operations Policy

## Membership lifecycle

Membership sales create a contract liability and a dated IFRS 15 recognition schedule. Freeze, extension, upgrade, cancellation and refund requests remain `SUBMITTED` until approved by a different authorized user. Approved changes rebuild only unrecognized schedules; recognized revenue is never silently rewritten.

## Refunds and member credit

Refunds may be paid through a bank account or credited to the member. Member credit is maintained in a debit/credit ledger and may be applied to a later contract change. The accounting entry reduces the member-credit liability and credits deferred revenue/output VAT as applicable. The same credit cannot be consumed twice.

## Classes

Each session has a branch, time, capacity and optional trainer. When full, eligible members enter a numbered waitlist. Cancelling a booked place promotes the earliest waitlisted booking automatically. Attendance is restricted to booked members.

## Personal training

A PT package sale posts cash, VAT and deferred revenue. Revenue is recognized only when a session is completed. Trainer commission is accrued from recognized session revenue, then paid through a prepare/review/approve process in which all three actors must be independent.

## Access and lockers

Access is granted only for a valid membership, within its effective dates, outside an approved freeze and at the assigned branch. Every attempt is retained as granted or denied with a reason. Lockers are uniquely controlled by branch and cannot be double-assigned. An approved branch transfer releases active lockers at the former branch.

## Audit and segregation

Critical changes use explicit statuses, immutable source references, user IDs and timestamps. A maker cannot approve their own membership modification or transfer, and a commission preparer/reviewer cannot approve the payout.
