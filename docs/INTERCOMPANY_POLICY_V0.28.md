# Intercompany Accounting and Reconciliation Policy

1. Each legal entity records its own transaction and accounting entry independently.
2. Records must identify the counterparty legal entity, document number, date, currency, account and local amount.
3. A match requires reciprocal entities and complementary directions: receivable/payable or revenue/expense.
4. Differences above the configured tolerance prevent matching and require investigation.
5. Matching does not alter either entity's statutory books.
6. Eliminations are created only in the consolidation layer.
7. Every elimination is linked to the source intercompany match and retained in the audit trail.
8. Unmatched balances remain visible in the reconciliation report and should block final group reporting when material.
