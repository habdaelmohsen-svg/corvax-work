- Member mobile/self-service booking, cancellation, waitlist and digital wallet experience.
- Add-on sales workflow for department access and automated package consumption.
- Padel leagues, tournaments, teams, scoring and coach allocation.
- Swimming operational controls such as lane programs, lifeguard rosters, water-quality logs and safety checklists.

- Physical access gate, QR reader, POS printer and payment-terminal integrations.
