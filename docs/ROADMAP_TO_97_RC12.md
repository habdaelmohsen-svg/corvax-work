# CORVAX — Remaining Roadmap from RC12 to 95–97% Internal Readiness

## Current position

The RC10 audit rated the internal system at approximately 92%, while HR, payroll, restaurants and gym operations were the weakest major domains. RC11 addressed the internal technical audit findings. RC12 materially completes HR/payroll and moves the overall internal estimate to approximately 93%.

## Next internal packages

### RC13 — Restaurant Operations & POS Completion

- table and reservation management;
- cashier shifts, opening/closing and variance controls;
- KDS order routing and preparation statuses;
- dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders;
- platform commissions and settlement reconciliation;
- returns, voids and approval controls;
- recipe/BOM, semi-finished items, waste and food-cost reconciliation;
- offline transaction queue and conflict handling foundation.

### RC14 — Gym Operations Completion

- membership lifecycle, freezing, extension, upgrade and cancellation;
- bookings, capacity and waiting list;
- PT packages, trainer sessions and commission approval;
- attendance and access records;
- lockers and branch transfers;
- IFRS 15 deferred revenue schedules for all membership changes;
- customer credit, refunds and payment reconciliation.

### RC15 — Enterprise UX, Reporting and Operational Depth

- full create/edit/approve forms for remaining modules;
- global search and saved filters;
- drill-down from statements and KPIs to source documents;
- unified Excel/PDF exports and print templates;
- responsive mobile/PWA hardening;
- ITSM and CRM workflow depth;
- role-based reports and notification center.

### RC16 — Manufacturing Completion and Production Hardening

- periodic multilevel actual-cost roll-up;
- subcontracting purchase workflow;
- by-products and complete rework inventory flows;
- alternate resources and parallel routing operations;
- MES/IoT adapter contracts;
- performance tests, PostgreSQL restore drill and migration toolkit.
