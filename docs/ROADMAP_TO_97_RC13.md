# CORVAX — Remaining Roadmap from RC13 to 95–97% Internal Readiness

## Current position

RC13 completes the principal backend control framework for restaurant operations and POS and raises the management estimate of overall internal readiness to approximately 94%. This does not change the external assurance and production-readiness restrictions.
