# CORVAX — Roadmap from RC14 to 97% Internal Readiness

## RC15 — Enterprise UX, Reporting and Operational Depth

- Complete data-entry/edit/review forms for remaining API-first workflows.
- Saved filters, global search, bulk actions, drill-down and role-specific work queues.
- Standardized print/PDF/Excel exports and management packs.
- Notification center, escalation rules and mobile/PWA field validation.
- Accessibility, Arabic RTL and English LTR regression across target devices.

## RC16 — Manufacturing Completion and Production Hardening

- Periodic actual-cost roll-up and multilevel variance analysis.
- Complete subcontracting, by-products, rework and alternate-resource workflows.
- Production capacity/performance testing and query optimization.
- PostgreSQL migration rehearsal, point-in-time recovery and signed restore drill.
- Data migration toolkit, reconciliation controls and deployment hardening.

## Production gate after internal completion

Production approval still requires independent security testing, company-data migration, departmental UAT, a full parallel reporting cycle, official integrations, hardware/provider certification, disaster-recovery evidence and external IFRS/actuarial validation where applicable.
