# CORVAX Roadmap from RC15 to 97% Internal Readiness

## RC16 — Enterprise UX, Reporting and Operational Depth

- Complete administration and transaction forms for RC15 gym departments/cafe and remaining API-led modules.
- Work queues, saved searches, bulk actions, notifications and approval inboxes.
- Report-to-document-to-journal drill-down.
- Unified export, printing and management packs.
- Mobile/tablet and full RTL/LTR acceptance remediation.
- Gym add-ons, digital wallet, self-service bookings, cafe promotions and specialist sports workflows.

## RC17 — Manufacturing and Production Hardening

- Advanced actual costing and variance analysis.
- Subcontracting, by-products, rework and alternate resources.
- Data migration and opening-balance reconciliation tools.
- PostgreSQL rehearsal, performance tuning, backup/restore and operational runbooks.
- Final internal regression and release-evidence pack.

External integrations, independent assurance and production acceptance are separate gates and are not counted as completed internal functionality.
