# CORVAX Business Platform v0.20 Status

## Added in v0.20
- IFRS 9 simplified expected credit loss engine.
- Configurable ageing buckets, loss rates and forward-looking factors.
- Credit exposure register and ECL run history.
- Optional automatic ECL journal posting.
- Maintenance asset register.
- Corrective and preventive maintenance work orders.
- Work-order start/completion workflow.
- Downtime, labor cost, spare-parts cost and MTTR dashboard.
- Audit trail and company-level permission checks.

## Current position
- Functional/demo coverage: 94%.
- Financial core readiness: 84%.
- Overall production readiness: 64%.

## Major remaining gaps
- Full IFRS 9 staging and PD/LGD/EAD models for loans and complex financial instruments.
- Maintenance preventive schedules, spare-parts reservations, calibration and MTBF.
- ZATCA production onboarding and CSID.
- Full consolidation including NCI, goodwill and acquisition accounting.
- Penetration, load, disaster-recovery and user-acceptance testing.
