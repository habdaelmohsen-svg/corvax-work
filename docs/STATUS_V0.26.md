# CORVAX Business Platform v0.26 — Accruals, Recurring Journals & Close Controls

## Implemented

- Persistent expense and revenue accrual register by company.
- Draft-to-post accrual lifecycle.
- Automatic future-period reversal journals.
- Accrued revenue current-asset account mapping.
- Accrued expenses current-liability account mapping.
- Monthly, quarterly and annual recurring journal templates.
- Due-run generation with duplicate prevention.
- Branch and cost-center allocation.
- Audit trail and company-level permissions.
- Blocking period-close controls for drafts, due reversals and recurring journals.
- Upgrade migration from v0.24 including permissions and account master additions.
- Arabic/English connected UI page.
