# CORVAX v1.0 RC14 — Gym Operations Completion Status

## Release position

RC14 is built directly on RC13 and preserves its restaurant/POS, finance, manufacturing, HR/payroll, governance and assurance capabilities. The release is suitable for staging, sanitized pilot and departmental UAT only.

- IFRS 15 schedule reconstruction for approved changes.
- Refund-to-bank and refund-to-member-credit accounting.
- Member ledger with reusable credit balance.
- Trainers, class types, class sessions, capacity and waitlists.
- Booking cancellation promotion and attendance capture.
- PT packages, sales, sessions and per-session revenue recognition.
- Trainer commission accrual and three-user payout controls.
- Membership-aware access decisions and retained denial reasons.
- Locker control and branch-transfer workflow.
- New permissions, Gym Manager and Gym Trainer role packs.
- Bilingual live-data gym dashboard.
