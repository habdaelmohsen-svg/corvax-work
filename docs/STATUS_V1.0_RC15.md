# CORVAX v1.0 RC15 Status

## Release scope

RC15 completes the first controlled operating layer for the gym's commercial departments and cafe. It is built directly on RC14 and preserves membership, IFRS 15, personal training, class, access, locker, restaurant, HR/payroll, finance and manufacturing capabilities.

## Delivered

- Multi-department gym master data with branch, type, capacity, cost center and revenue account.
- Plan-to-department access policies: included, add-on, pay-per-use or excluded.
- Department access validation, monthly visit limits and denial audit trail.
- Sports facilities and bookings with capacity, availability, overlap and maintenance-state controls.
- Paid-booking maker/approver workflow, VAT accounting and refund reversal.
- Department/facility linkage for trainers, class types and class sessions.
- Gym cafe product catalogue with member prices and nutritional/allergen data.
- POS cafe sales with recipes, stock consumption, VAT, cost of sales and gross profit.
- Independent business-unit reporting for restaurant and gym cafe.
- Gym commercial dashboard fed from live APIs.

## Internal maturity estimate

- Gym memberships and core operations: 90–93%.
- Gym departments, facilities and cafe: 92–95%.
- Combined gym business line: approximately 93–95%.
- Overall internal CORVAX scope: approximately 95%.

These percentages are management estimates and are not independent assurance.

## Qualification

Release candidate for development, staging, sanitized pilot and UAT only. Production acceptance still requires real-company data, operational sign-off, device/provider tests, security testing, performance testing and a parallel financial cycle.
