# RC24 Zakat and Corporate Income Tax Preparation Policy

1. The engine is a controlled preparation and reconciliation workbench, not an automatic legal determination.
2. The posted general ledger is the only accounting-profit source.
3. Zakat and CIT adjustments require a code, description, amount and optional source-account/evidence reference.
4. Ownership percentages must total 100%.
5. Tax-loss utilization is controlled by the taxpayer-profile cap and available approved losses.
6. The configured Hijri zakat rate is prorated by fiscal-period days over the configured Hijri-year days.
7. A preparer or submitter cannot approve the same return.
8. Approval creates separate Zakat and current-income-tax expense/payable entries.
9. Payment clears the liabilities through the selected bank account and retains SADAD references.
10. Taxpayer-specific legal interpretation, ZATCA filing and independent review remain mandatory before production use.
